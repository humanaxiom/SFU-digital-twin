  artifact fields. Keep live-status integration, travel-time estimates, alias ingestion, and additional
  source records in separate increments.

## Facilities records to request next

Request a sample of the manual restricted-area designations with stable IDs,
floor and geometry, the reason for the designation, its owner and any effective
or expiry dates. Compare it to the pathways in an isolated candidate. A missing
designation leaves access unknown. Ask Facilities whether Security is the right
contact for building schedules and locks. Outage notices in SNAP and at elevators
have no confirmed integration contract.

Request a sample of the automatic-door locations with room or floor keys and
record dates. A mapped automatic door is a useful landmark after validation.
Routing through it would also need a verified door-to-path relationship and
evidence for the claimed access conditions. Defer travel-time estimates and
survey-dependent routing until suitable measurements and authority are available.
