# DT-022 — Exact source-vertex topology and usable routes

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: isolated candidate verified; not promoted.

The AQ6071 screenshot exposed a graph-construction defect. The accepted endpoint-only
importer treated a pathway endpoint as disconnected when it landed exactly on an
interior vertex of another source pathway. For example, legacy feature 16548 starts
at the same level and exact XYZ coordinate as an interior vertex of feature 9166.
This is source-authored topology and does not require an inferred proximity edge.

ADR-0015 adds an opt-in `exact-shared-vertices-v1` mode. It splits source lines only
at exact same-level XYZ vertices, preserves the accepted endpoint node identities,
allocates authoritative `LENGTH_3D` proportionally across native source segments,
and records source feature/span provenance on every piece. Multipart records remain
as inherited graph geometry but are excluded from this noding pass. Ambiguous 1 cm
snap-cell collisions fail closed and remain audited. The default
`endpoint-v1` behavior is unchanged.

Two complete source-to-candidate builds used the same frozen source and code:
`dt022-endpoint-final3` and `dt022-exact-final3`. Their sealed manifest hashes are
`8c02ae5bb2563c4b371dc11c5bfc1f7d713379e5be52b0287dbcea7dcbaae227` and
`4b2b16807a2502630d4b52a4a904d65ec5c2db0f5bee9051c1de672d5add81e6`.
The candidate audit reports 1,999 split features, 8,507 physical pieces, 4,205
junctions and 2,203 new nodes. Raw directed edges increase from 44,826 to 57,842.
Eighteen features remain excluded: 12 inherited multipart records and six inherited
self-loops. Eighty-five unsafe junctions are skipped. There are zero metric mismatches.
The guard indexes both native-rounded coordinates and the actual sealed legacy
endpoint/transition node cells. A synthetic half-centimetre-boundary regression
previously created a false join; the corrected guard skips it. A full real-candidate
probe found no such incompatible occupied cell among the 4,205 accepted junctions.

Independent reconstruction from the GeoPackage and topology audit verified all
1,999 split features: exact source-coordinate slices, one forward/reverse arc pair
per piece, complete ordered source-segment coverage and total authoritative-length
conservation against the GeoPackage `LENGTH_3D` field. Corruption tests fail for
altered geometry, weight, missing arcs, and coordinated graph/audit weight changes.

AQ6071 changes from one connected destination to 696 under both profiles. The
candidate-backed browser/API gate passed 11 generated and canonical fixtures at
1440×1000 and 390×844, plus 320px layout coverage. It verifies same-floor and
cross-floor gained routes, initial route framing, explicit floor departure/arrival,
plain-language instructions and exact selected-step path highlighting. Evidence is
in `build/experiments/evaluations/dt022-final3.json` and
`build/takeover-browser/dt022-candidate-final3/`.

The client also clears a stale failure when a new origin is chosen. When only one
to five destinations are connected it presents named “Directions to …” actions;
larger connected sets stay in the mapped destination selector. A fresh browser/API
run against the unchanged accepted graph passed all canonical fixtures and the
AQ6071 stale-error/named-AQ6067 action at desktop and mobile widths.

The accepted artifacts and port-18087 stack were not replaced. The candidate is
served separately for review. Route geometry still begins and ends at approximate
room anchors; door connections, closures, elevator status and physical accessibility
remain unverified. This topology correction does not resolve the inherited multipart
source defects or certify publication and physical LAN use.
